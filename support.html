How do I add a camera or lens?

On the Collection screen, tap the add button. Enter the equipment information, add a photo if you like, and save the record.

How can I add more photos?

Open an item for editing and use the photo controls to take or select additional photographs. Images are compressed to save device storage space.

How do I link equipment?

Edit an item and use Related Equipment to associate cameras, lenses and accessories in your collection. Links are selected by you and do not automatically verify compatibility.

How do I change currency?

Choose a default currency in Settings. Individual equipment records can use a different currency; changing your default does not convert existing amounts.

How do I protect my collection?

Use the app's backup/export feature and store a copy somewhere safe before deleting or reinstalling the app. Check that you can restore your backup before relying on it.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Camera Collection version and build number, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful, but remove private serial numbers or other information you don't want to share.
