Camera Collection

Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Camera Collection is developed by Christopher Parrey. This policy describes how the app and this website handle information.

Information stored on your device

The app lets you save equipment names, models, serial numbers, purchase details, values, currencies, photos, notes, related equipment and other collection details. These records are stored locally on your iPhone. An account is not required.

Data collection and sharing

Camera Collection does not include advertising or third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. The app is not designed to transmit your collection records or photographs to the developer or to an application-operated server. We do not sell your data.

Camera and photo access

With your permission, the app uses your device camera to take equipment photographs. You can also select photos using Apple's system photo picker. Selected images are resized and compressed on-device for storage in your collection. You control whether to add photos. Camera permission can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Backups and exports

You may choose to export a collection backup. You control where exported files are saved or shared. Files placed in iCloud Drive or other third-party storage are then subject to those services' privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Records stay on your device until removed in the app or deleted with its local data. Removing the app may delete local equipment records and photos; export a backup first if you want to preserve them. Apple device backups or offloaded-app data may retain copies according to your system settings.

Support enquiries

If you email customlightmeter@gmail.com, your message, email address and any attachments you voluntarily supply are used to answer your enquiry. Email is processed by the relevant email providers. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Website

This site does not run advertising or visitor-tracking scripts. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may process network and request data needed to operate and secure the website.

Changes and contact

This policy may change if the app's features or data practices change. Questions can be sent to customlightmeter@gmail.com.
